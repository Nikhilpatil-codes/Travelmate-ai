How TravelMate Works

TravelMate AI evaluates every Karnataka destination against your real inputs using deterministic multi-criteria decision analysis across 8 weighted factors:

20%
Budget Match: Estimated group cost vs your budget (₹500 - ₹50,000)
20%
Interests Alignment: Direct overlap with destination tags & activities
15%
Available Time: Recommended duration matched to your trip window
15%
Travel Mood: Relaxation, adventure, family, romantic or photography
10%
Food Preference: Pure veg dining availability and local food options
10%
Distance (Haversine): Strict cutoff filter + proximity bonus
5%
Transportation: Route accessibility for car, bike, bus, or train
5%
Travel Period: Peak season vs off-season best month rating
